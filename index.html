WHO WE ARE

Built around radio and fellowship.

Red Dirt Road Rangers brings together operators who enjoy amateur radio in the shack, on the road, and out in the field.

Experienced operators, new hams, families, experimenters, builders, portable operators, DXers and contesters are welcome.

More than one way to radio.

POTA

02

Summits on the Air, portable operating, lightweight stations and chasing activators.

03

DX

Propagation, antennas, rare stations, awards and improving our stations.

04

On-air gatherings for fellowship, training, announcements and experimentation.
